How to actually study this material

Five habits that outperform re-reading

  • Spaced repetition — review material at increasing intervals instead of cramming; flashcard apps like Quizlet do this automatically if you use their spaced-repetition study modes.
  • Practice testing over re-reading — pulling an answer out of memory (even wrong) builds stronger recall than reading it again.
  • Interleave your topics — mix cardiac, trauma, and medical questions in one session instead of studying one topic to exhaustion.
  • Practice the skill, not just the fact — rhythm recognition and med dosing are motor-memory-adjacent skills; use a simulator, not just flashcards.
  • Explain it out loud — if you can't teach a concept to a study partner in plain language, you don't know it yet.
