Finding source material

Where to find citable cases

Build the case library from sources that can be checked by readers rather than from anonymous internet anecdotes or reconstructed scenarios presented as real events.

  • Search peer-reviewed EMS and emergency medicine literature for published case reports, reviews, and documented prehospital cases.
  • Use PubMed and PubMed Central to locate peer-reviewed articles and freely available full-text material.
  • Professional EMS organizations and state EMS authorities may publish de-identified educational case reviews and incident analyses.
  • Agency quality-improvement or case-review material can be educational when it has been appropriately de-identified and cleared for external publication.
Important information

Case studies are educational material

Published cases describe individual patients or events and should not be treated as universal treatment protocols. Clinical decisions should follow current EMS education, applicable state and local requirements, agency protocols, medical direction, and current evidence.

Patient information should be presented only when appropriate and should be de-identified whenever possible. Do not publish protected or identifying information without the necessary authorization.
