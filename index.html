```
What we're about

Advancing medicine through education, research, and responsible AI

We are an emerging nonprofit focused on the intersection of emergency medicine, healthcare, education, and artificial intelligence. We believe that new technology should be developed with clinicians, evaluated scientifically, and made accessible to the people who can use it to learn, improve care, and advance medicine.

Our work brings together educational resources, clinical tools, artificial intelligence research, and real-world evaluation. We aim to create and support technologies that are useful in practice—not simply impressive in demonstration.

Our mission

Research with purpose. Innovation with responsibility.

To advance emergency medicine and healthcare through the development, testing, and scientific evaluation of artificial intelligence systems, while making research and educational tools accessible to clinicians, students, and the public.

We advance artificial intelligence in medicine through research, publication, education, and responsible innovation. Our goal is to help bridge the gap between emerging technology and the people who need trustworthy, understandable, evidence-informed tools.

```
```
01 — Research

Study what works

We develop and scientifically evaluate AI systems with an emphasis on reproducibility, meaningful outcomes, and real-world clinical applications.

02 — Education

Make knowledge accessible

We create free educational resources and tools for clinicians, students, educators, and members of the public who want to better understand medicine and emerging healthcare technology.

03 — Responsible innovation

Build technology thoughtfully

We believe medical AI should be developed transparently, evaluated critically, and used responsibly—with patient safety, clinical judgment, and scientific integrity at the center.
```
