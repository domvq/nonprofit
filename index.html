What we're about

Building A Bridge Between Medicine and Emerging Technology.

We are an organization focused on the intersection of emergency medicine, healthcare, artificial intelligence, research, and education.

We believe new technology should be developed with clinicians, tested scientifically, evaluated honestly, and made accessible to the people who can benefit from it.

01 — RESEARCH

Study what works

We explore and scientifically evaluate artificial intelligence systems and their potential applications in healthcare.

02 — EDUCATION

Make knowledge accessible

We create educational resources that help clinicians, students, and the public understand medicine and emerging technology.

03 — INNOVATION

Build responsibly

We believe healthcare AI should be developed with transparency, clinical context, scientific integrity, and patient safety in mind.

Our mission

Research With Purpose. Innovation With Responsibility.

Our mission is to advance emergency medicine and healthcare by exploring how artificial intelligence can be developed, tested, evaluated, and responsibly applied.

We also believe that research and education should not be limited to people who can afford expensive platforms or proprietary tools.

To advance emergency medicine and healthcare through the development, testing, and scientific evaluation of artificial intelligence systems, while making research and educational tools accessible to clinicians, students, and the public.


We advance artificial intelligence in medicine through research, publication, education, and responsible innovation.

AI & Research

From Promising Technology to Evidence.

Artificial intelligence is changing healthcare rapidly. We believe the next step is determining which systems are actually useful, reliable, safe, and worth using.

Development Developing AI systems for healthcare education and research.
Testing Testing systems against defined tasks and real-world requirements.
Scientific Evaluation Measuring whether technology actually provides meaningful value.
Publication Sharing research and making findings accessible and understandable.
Responsible AI

Build it. Test it. Question it.

Healthcare AI cannot be evaluated simply by how impressive a demonstration looks.

We prioritize transparency, scientific evaluation, clinical context, limitations, and responsible use.

Our goal is to help distinguish what AI can do from what it has actually been demonstrated to do.

EMS Education

Education Is Where Our Work Begins.

Our EMS education resources are designed to support learners throughout their journey—from the fundamentals of emergency care through advanced clinical concepts.

EMR

Emergency Medical Responder

Foundational emergency care concepts and preparation for entering EMS.

EMT

Emergency Medical Technician

Patient assessment, treatment, anatomy, physiology, operations, and exam preparation.

AEMT

Advanced EMT

Advanced assessment, pharmacology, procedures, and clinical decision-making concepts.
