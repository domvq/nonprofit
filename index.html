What we do

Engineering and evaluating AI for emergency medicine.

EMARI works at the intersection of emergency medicine, healthcare and artificial intelligence. We study where AI can support prehospital care, build prototypes to test those ideas, and report honestly on how they perform.

01 — RESEARCH

Study what works

We investigate where AI can help in emergency and prehospital medicine, and where it fails, using defined tasks and simulated or de-identified data.

02 — ENGINEERING

Build to a defined task

We engineer prototypes for ECG interpretation, vital-sign monitoring, trauma assessment and clinical documentation against explicit requirements, not demos.

03 — EVALUATION

Test before deploying

We measure performance, document limitations, and publish code, test sets and written reports so others can reproduce and challenge the work.

Research program

From promising technology to evidence.

Artificial intelligence is changing healthcare quickly. The next step is determining which systems are useful, reliable, safe, and worth using.

DevelopmentDeveloping AI systems for emergency medicine and prehospital care.
EngineeringBuilding systems to defined tasks and real-world requirements.
Scientific evaluationMeasuring whether a system provides meaningful value, and documenting how it fails.
PublicationReleasing code, prototypes, datasets and reports. Read our methods →
Responsible AI

Build it. Test it. Question it. Then deploy it.

Healthcare AI cannot be judged by how impressive a demonstration looks.

We prioritize transparency, scientific evaluation and clinical context before anything reaches production.

Our goal is to show what AI can do in emergency medicine, and where it cannot yet be trusted.
